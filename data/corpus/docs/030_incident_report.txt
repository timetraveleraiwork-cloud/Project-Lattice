Incident Report - Critical Server Outage (LDPL-SRV-2024-007)

**INCIDENT REPORT**Lattice Dynamics Pvt. Ltd.Incident ID: INC-LDPL-2024-007Date of Report: October 26, 20241. Incident Overview   Time of Discovery: October 25, 2024, 09:15 AM IST   Time of Resolution: October 25, 2024, 01:45 PM IST   Total Downtime: 4 hours 30 minutes   Incident Type: Critical System Outage - Core Infrastructure (Cloud-hosted VM)2. Affected Systems & Impact   *   Systems Affected: ERP System, Internal Project Management Tool, Data Analytics Platform.   *   Impact on Operations: Significant disruption across multiple departments including Finance, Procurement, and Operations. Employees were unable to access critical business applications, leading to a standstill in daily tasks. [] Potential delay in Q3 financial data aggregation for reporting purposes was noted.3. Initial Discovery & Reporting   The outage was first reported by Neha Singh (Data Analyst) at approximately 09:15 AM on October 25, 2024, due to her inability to access the Data Analytics Platform. Rahul Sharma (IT Administrator) was alerted and immediately began investigation, confirming a complete outage of the primary virtual machine hosted by CloudKart Services.4. Root Cause Analysis   Preliminary investigation, in conjunction with CloudKart Services, revealed that the primary VM instance unexpectedly shut down during an unannounced host-level maintenance window. CloudKart Services initiated a routine patching cycle on their physical host infrastructure, which led to an unforeseen failure and subsequent abrupt termination of our dedicated server. Critically, CloudKart Services failed to provide prior notification as explicitly stipulated in our Service Level Agreement (SLA) (Contract ID: CK-LDPL-2022-001). This breach of protocol directly contributed to the severity and duration of the incident.5. Resolution Steps   *   09:30 AM, Oct 25: Rahul Sharma opened a critical support ticket (Ticket #CK-98765) with CloudKart Services.   *   10:00 AM, Oct 25: Ankit Gupta (Support Engineer) commenced contingency operations, preparing to restore services from recent off-site backups while awaiting vendor response.   *   11:00 AM, Oct 25: CloudKart Services confirmed the issue was on their end, a host-level failure, and began restoration efforts, prioritizing our instance due to SLA violation.   *   01:15 PM, Oct 25: Primary ERP system and associated services were brought back online and verified stable by Rahul Sharma.   *   01:45 PM, Oct 25: Communication disseminated to all affected departments, confirming full service restoration.6. Preventative Measures & Recommendations   To mitigate future risks and prevent recurrence, the following actions are recommended:   *   SLA Review: Meera Iyer (Procurement Lead) to immediately initiate a review and renegotiation of our SLA with CloudKart Services, ensuring stricter notification protocols for all maintenance activities, scheduled or unscheduled. This should include penalty clauses for non-compliance. []   *   Enhanced Monitoring: Rahul Sharma will lead a project to implement advanced proactive monitoring tools capable of detecting host-level anomalies on cloud infrastructure more swiftly. ETA for proposal: Nov 15, 2024.   *   Vendor Post-Mortem: A formal post-incident review meeting with CloudKart Services is to be scheduled. Sandeep Menon (Compliance Officer) and Priya Nair (Finance Manager) will attend to address compliance and financial implications of the service interruption. Vikram Patel (Executive Director) to oversee this.   *   Internal Training: Refresh internal IT staff training on emergency backup and recovery procedures for critical systems, focusing on faster manual failover options.7.
