Employee Spotlight: Priya Nair, Finance Manager

Welcome to another edition of our Employee Spotlight series! Today, we're thrilled to feature Priya Nair, our dedicated Finance Manager. Priya joined Lattice Dynamics Pvt. Ltd. in January 2018, bringing with her a wealth of experience in corporate finance and accounting from her previous roles. Before her tenure here, Priya honed her skills at a leading financial services firm, specializing in budget analysis, financial reporting, and risk assessment. Her expertise was immediately evident as she quickly integrated into our Finance Department, streamlining several key processes and introducing best practices for financial controls. Over the past six years, Priya has been instrumental in optimizing our financial operations. She spearheaded the implementation of our new expense management system in FY2020, which significantly reduced processing times by an estimated 25% and improved data accuracy by minimizing manual entry errors. Her meticulous approach to financial planning and forecasting has been crucial in navigating various economic shifts and market volatilities, ensuring Lattice Dynamics maintains a strong, resilient fiscal position. She frequently collaborates with Meera Iyer in Procurement and the Operations team to identify and implement cost efficiencies across various projects, ensuring optimal resource utilization. Some of Priya’s key contributions include: • Leading the annual budget preparation and review cycles for the entire organization, presenting complex financial scenarios to the Executive Office. • Successfully negotiating favorable payment terms and pricing with key vendors, including TechNova Solutions Pvt Ltd for our enterprise software licensing, resulting in substantial annual savings.  • Developing comprehensive financial models and dashboards that provide critical insights, directly supporting strategic decision-decision-making by senior leadership, often working closely with Vikram Patel. • Mentoring junior staff within the Finance department, fostering a culture of continuous learning, professional growth, and promoting best financial practices. She's always ready to offer guidance. Priya is known for her exceptional analytical skills, her keen eye for detail, and her remarkable ability to simplify complex financial data into actionable insights for non-finance stakeholders. Her colleagues often praise her collaborative spirit and her readiness to assist with ad-hoc requests, always striving for EOD completion on critical tasks. She frequently liaises with Sandeep Menon in Compliance to ensure all financial activities adhere to regulatory standards and internal policies. Outside of her demanding role, Priya is an avid reader, particularly enjoying historical fiction, and enjoys hiking expeditions during weekends. Her unwavering commitment to excellence, her strategic financial acumen, and her dedicated team-player attitude make her an invaluable asset to Lattice Dynamics. We are incredibly fortunate to have Priya on our team! Her hard work truly exemplifies our company values, especially integrity and innovation.
