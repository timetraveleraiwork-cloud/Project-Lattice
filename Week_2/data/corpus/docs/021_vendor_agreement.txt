Vendor Agreement: IT Infrastructure Support & Software Licensing

Contract ID: LD-VN-TN-2024-001. Effective Date: June 10, 2024. Renewal Date: June 09, 2025. Parties: Lattice Dynamics Pvt. Ltd. (Client), located at Corporate Tower, 15th Floor, Mumbai, India, represented by Vikram Patel, Executive Director. TechNova Solutions Pvt Ltd (Vendor), located at Tech Hub Park, Building 7, Bengaluru, India, represented by Rajesh Kumar, Managing Director. Preamble: This Vendor Agreement (Agreement) outlines the terms and conditions under which TechNova Solutions Pvt Ltd will provide IT Infrastructure Support and Software Licensing Services to Lattice Dynamics Pvt. Ltd. Article 1: Scope of Services: The Vendor shall provide comprehensive IT infrastructure support including, but not limited to, network maintenance, server management, and end-user technical assistance. Additionally, the Vendor will facilitate the procurement and licensing of specific software solutions as detailed in Schedule A [Services Scope]. This encompasses regular system audits and proactive monitoring to ensure optimal performance for Lattice Dynamics' IT Department operations. Attachments detailing specific software lists are PFA with this agreement. Article 2: Term and Termination: This Agreement is effective from the “Effective Date” and shall continue for an initial period of twelve (12) months, unless terminated earlier as per the provisions herein. Either party may terminate this Agreement with thirty (30) days’ written notice for material breach of terms or for convenience with ninety (90) days’ notice. Renewal will be automatic unless a written notice of non-renewal is provided to Meera Iyer, Procurement Lead, 60 days prior to the “Renewal Date”. Article 3: Payment Terms: Lattice Dynamics Pvt. Ltd. agrees to compensate TechNova Solutions Pvt Ltd as per the payment schedule outlined in Schedule B. Invoices will be submitted monthly, due Net 30 days from receipt. All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. For any billing queries or clarifications, please contact Priya Nair, Finance Manager, directly. Article 4: Confidentiality: Both parties commit to maintaining strict confidentiality regarding all proprietary and sensitive information exchanged during the course of this Agreement. This obligation shall survive the termination of this Agreement. Any data accessed by the Vendor will be handled in strict accordance with Lattice Dynamics' data privacy policies, overseen by Sandeep Menon, Compliance Officer. Article 5: Service Level Agreement (SLA): Specific service levels, including response times and uptime guarantees, are meticulously detailed in Schedule C (PFA). The Vendor commits to meeting or exceeding these agreed-upon standards. Rahul Sharma, IT Administrator, will serve as the primary contact for performance monitoring and operational liaison. Article 6: Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes arising hereunder shall first be resolved through amicable discussion; failing which, through arbitration in Mumbai, India. Signatories: For and on behalf of Lattice Dynamics Pvt.
