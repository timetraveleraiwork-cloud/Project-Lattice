Project Memo: SecureIT Infrastructure Upgrade - Phase I Commencement

TO: Vikram Patel (Executive Director), Priya Nair (Finance Manager), Sandeep Menon (Compliance Officer)FROM: Arjun Verma (Project Manager)DATE: September 05, 2024SUBJECT: SecureIT Infrastructure Upgrade - Phase I Commencement & OverviewProject Name: SecureIT Infrastructure Upgrade - Phase IIntroduction & Objectives:This memo serves to officially announce the commencement of Phase I of the "SecureIT Infrastructure Upgrade" project. The primary objective is to significantly enhance Lattice Dynamics Pvt. Ltd.'s core IT infrastructure, focusing on network security, data integrity, and scalable cloud solutions. This upgrade is critical for improving operational resilience, meeting evolving compliance standards, and supporting our strategic growth initiatives. Our aim is to minimize vulnerabilities and ensure robust data protection across all departments.Key Team Members:* Arjun Verma - Project Manager (Operations Department)* Rahul Sharma - IT Administrator (IT Department - Lead Technical Architect)* Ankit Gupta - Support Engineer (IT Department - Implementation Support)* Neha Singh - Data Analyst (IT Department - Data Migration & Validation)* Sandeep Menon - Compliance Officer (Compliance Department - Advisory & Policy Review)Project Scope - Phase I:Phase I will concentrate on two critical areas:1. Network Security Enhancement: Deployment of advanced firewall solutions and intrusion detection systems from SecureNet Systems.2. Initial Cloud Migration: Migration of select non-critical data storage and specific application workloads to CloudKart Services to assess performance and integration.Deadlines:* Kick-off Meeting: September 09, 2024* SecureNet Systems Deployment Completion: October 15, 2024* CloudKart Services Initial Migration Complete: October 31, 2024 (Target for Phase I completion)* Phase I Review & Go/No-Go for Phase II: November 08, 2024The overall project is slated for full deployment by January 31, 2025.Budget Overview (Phase I):An initial budget allocation of INR 2,500,000 has been approved for Phase I.This allocation primarily covers:* SecureNet Systems - Hardware & Software Licenses: INR 1,200,000* CloudKart Services - Setup & Initial Subscription: INR 800,000* Contingency & Miscellaneous [Training, Documentation]: INR 500,000FYI: A detailed budget breakdown is attached for your review.
